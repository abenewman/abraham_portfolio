## The idea

This is placeholder copy, but the shape of it is real. Start with the seed of the project: the image, overheard line, or stubborn question that would not leave you alone. For this one it was a photograph of a truck stop at four in the morning, every booth full and nobody talking to anybody.

The script grew out of a simple constraint. Two people, one cab, eleven hundred miles, and a deadline that neither of them can negotiate with. Everything they have avoided saying for nineteen years has to fit inside that box, and the box keeps moving.

Swap this text for your own. Three or four paragraphs is the sweet spot - enough to show how you think, short enough that a reader who is skimming twenty portfolios still finishes it.

> The road film is the only genre where the destination is allowed to be a disappointment. That is the whole reason it works.

## How it was built
