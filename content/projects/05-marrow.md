## Why comics

Placeholder copy. When the form is unusual for your field, justify it. Why is this a graphic novel and not a screenplay?

Because the story is about a face assembling itself one decision at a time, and comics are the only medium where the reader controls how long they sit with an image. A film would make that choice for them.

## Writing for an artist

Talk about craft specific to the form. Comics scripts are a handoff document, so the interesting question is how much you specify and how much you leave open.

Describe the convention you settled on, and what you learned from the pages that came back looking nothing like what you pictured - usually better.
