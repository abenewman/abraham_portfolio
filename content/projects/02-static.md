## Overview

In the Manufactured Side, an artificial civilization devoid of nature and divided by wealth, a forbidden wall of violent storms looms over daily life. Mara, a young thief, is hired to rob Ren, an idealistic boy from a powerful family.

When the theft draws them toward the Stormwall and reveals clues to a shared past, the unlikely pair cross into a magnificent living world. There, they discover they are twins, born in this paradise and sent to the Manufactured Side before being accidentally separated. They also uncover evidence that Meridian Systems, the corporation controlling their entire society, has concealed the living world’s existence.

Reunited, Mara and Ren must decide whether to reveal this paradise to a society desperate for renewal or protect it from the exploitation that could follow. But with Meridian Systems closing in, their chance to choose is slipping away.
