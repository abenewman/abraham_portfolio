I grew up on California’s Central Coast in San Luis Obispo County, where I spent much of my childhood reading through the fantasy and science fiction shelves at my local library. When I ran out of stories to read, I began writing my own. What started as poems and short stories quickly grew into my first major project: a five-book high fantasy series that I’m still developing.

Fantasy remains at the heart of my writing because it allows me to do what I love most: build worlds. Michelangelo famously said, "Every block of stone has a statue inside it, and it is the task of the sculptor to discover it," and as writers, it is similarly our job to simply find the stories contained within worlds. I believe that there are countless worlds waiting to be discovered, and each one has a multitude of stories worth telling and numerous characters who bring those stories to life. 

I also love discovering worlds and crafting stories with other people. I’ve collaborated with artists on children’s picture books and developed worlds and characters alongside several friends. In 2026, I began pursuing my interest in animation more deliberately by writing feature screenplays, completing script coverages, and developing adaptation proposals. These projects have helped me bring my love of worldbuilding into a more visual, collaborative form of storytelling.

I hope to work on animated features at DreamWorks Animation or Walt Disney Animation Studios. The expansive worlds of DreamWorks feature films, such as How to Train Your Dragon and Shrek have long inspired my fascination with worldbuilding, while Disney’s animated musical storytelling speaks to my lifelong love of music in tandem with narrative.
